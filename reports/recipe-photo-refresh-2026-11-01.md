## Lentil stew prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: high resolution food photograph for the mobile meal planning app Miga. Create a single warm, natural editorial photograph of a rustic cream ceramic bowl of tomato lentil stew, cooked brown lentils clearly visible (small flat lentils, NOT chickpeas), diced carrots, tomato, onion and olive oil, a little parsley. Comforting ordinary home cooked meal. Landscape 4:3 composition, camera at a 45 degree slightly overhead angle. Entire bowl clearly visible with at least 12 percent space around ALL edges, placed in the center of the image. Simple warm wood and cream linen tabletop, soft daylight from side, crisp detailed food texture, realistic natural color and restrained styling. The food fills central 65 percent so it can crop safely to landscape and portrait recipe cards. No people, no hands, no writing, no logos, no watermark, no collage. Main subject is the meal. Produce one photo.

## Chicken and potatoes prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: high resolution food photograph for the mobile meal planning app Miga. Create a single warm, natural editorial photograph of a cream ceramic platter with golden oven baked boneless chicken breast pieces, roasted potatoes, carrots and onion, seasoned with olive oil and a few herbs. An ordinary appetizing family meal, NOT a whole bird and NOT a festive table. Landscape 4:3 composition, camera at a 45 degree slightly overhead angle. Entire platter visible with at least 12 percent space around ALL edges, centered horizontally AND vertically. Simple warm wood and cream linen tabletop, soft natural daylight, crisp detailed textures, realistic natural colors. Food fills central 65 percent so landscape and portrait crop both keep chicken and potatoes visible. No large empty background above the food, no bokeh lights, no people, no hands, no writing, no logos, no watermark, no collage. Produce one photo.
