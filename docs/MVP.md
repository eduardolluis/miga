## Shopping discovery

Miga helps users find nearby stores that may carry items from their shopping list.

### Planned capabilities

- Show nearby grocery stores on a map.
- Let users compare available prices for shopping list items.
- Highlight stores with lower prices when reliable price data is available.

### Initial limitation

Store locations and prices require reliable data. The MVP may start with nearby store locations and allow users to add or report prices. Price comparisons will be shown only when enough current information is available.
