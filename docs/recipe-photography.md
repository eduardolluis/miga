# Fotografía de recetas

- Archivo usado por la aplicación: [`assets/images/home/recipes-casual.png`](../assets/images/home/recipes-casual.png).
- Herramienta: `imagegen` integrada, generación de imagen nueva.
- Composición: nueve fotos en una cuadrícula de 3 × 3; `RecipeImage` muestra únicamente la celda de cada plato. El recorte se hace al renderizar en Flutter.
- Dirección visual: fotos de estilo cotidiano, luz difusa, enfoque suave, sin microtexturas exageradas ni acabado publicitario.

## Prompt final

> Create one square 1536 by 1536 pixel image containing a precise 3 by 3 grid of nine separate casual smartphone food photographs. Each square photograph must extend exactly to its tile edges: NO borders, NO gaps, NO labels, NO typography. These are ordinary home-cooked meals casually photographed in a real everyday kitchen, not food advertising. Soft overcast daylight, gentle focus, slight natural phone grain, restrained color, ordinary plain white or simple ceramic plates and a normal tabletop. Slightly imperfect portions and casual framing. NO highly sharpened microtexture, NO HDR, NO cinematic lighting, NO glossy surfaces, NO artificial studio props, NO painstaking garnish, NO styled linen, no impossible ingredients. Food should look inviting but normal and homemade. Row 1 left: a simple bowl of banana oatmeal with a few walnuts; row 1 middle: avocado toast with scrambled eggs and a few tomatoes; row 1 right: plain yogurt with strawberry, mango and a little granola. Row 2 left: a modest baked salmon fillet with lemon, baby potatoes and broccoli; row 2 middle: cooked chicken pieces with rice, carrots and broccoli in a bowl; row 2 right: a simple chickpea salad with avocado, tomato and cucumber. Row 3 left: an everyday plate of tomato and basil pasta; row 3 middle: a homemade bowl of sweet potato cubes and cooked spinach topped with a cooked egg; row 3 right: lentil soup with carrots and vegetables. Each tile is a separate photograph of only its own specified dish, centered so it can be cropped to a wide horizontal card. Keep the overall effect relaxed, believable and softly photographed, not hyperreal or AI-polished.
